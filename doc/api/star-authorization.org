* Variables and Constants
*** *authorization-audit-sink*
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: var
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Function called with every authorization audit event.
*** *current-authorization-decision*
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: var
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Dynamic binding of the in-flight authorization decision.
*** *policy-engine*
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: var
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Global policy engine consulted for every authorization check.
*** *trusted-authorization-context*
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: var
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Dynamic binding for service-to-service trusted contexts.
*** +capabilities+
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: var
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Closed capability vocabulary. Unknown capabilities are rejected at issuance.
*** +scope-prefixes+
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: var
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Closed resource-scope vocabulary. Values follow the prefix and may be `*`.

* Types and Classes
*** default-deny-policy-engine
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: class
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Policy engine that denies unless a rule explicitly allows.
*** policy-engine
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: class
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Protocol object answering authorization questions.
*** authorization-decision
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: struct
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Result of one authorization check: allow/deny plus reasons.
- Slots: =id=, =allowed-p=, =reason=, =action=, =resource=, =principal-id=
*** authorization-request
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: struct
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
One authorization question: principal, action, resource.
- Slots: =principal=, =action=, =resource=, =metadata=, =quotas=
*** authorization-resource
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: struct
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
The resource side of an authorization request.
- Slots: =tenant-id=, =dataset-id=, =actor-name=, =target-id=, =target-namespace=, =program-id=, =resource-id=, =dtype=
*** trusted-authorization-context
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: struct
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Pre-authenticated context for internal call chains.
- Slots: =id=, =principal-type=, =scopes=

* Conditions
*** authorization-error
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: condition
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
Signalled for malformed authorization requests.
- Slots: =code=, =decision=

*** authorized-update-document
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: fn
:PACKAGE: STAR.AUTHORIZATION
:END:
#+begin_src lisp
(authorized-update-document (DOCUMENT-ID PATCH FETCH-FN UPDATE-FN &KEY PRINCIPAL METADATA))
#+end_src
Authorize an update against the current document before persistence.

FETCH-FN returns the current document or NIL for an insert candidate. UPDATE-FN
receives PATCH only after the resource decision has been made.
